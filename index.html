Jarvis Workspace

Read-only Google Workspace assistance for Andrew Barber through a privately controlled Hermes Agent installation.

Purpose

Jarvis helps Andrew understand schedules, communications, documents, contacts, and other personal information when he requests assistance. It is a private integration for personal use and is not offered as a public service.

Access

Jarvis requests read-only access to Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts data available through the Google account that authorizes it.

These permissions do not allow Jarvis to send email or create, change, share, or delete Google Workspace content.

Use

Google user data is accessed only to provide assistance requested by Andrew, such as reviewing calendar commitments, locating information, summarizing relevant material, and supporting personal planning.

Privacy

How Google user data is accessed, processed, stored, and removed is described in the Jarvis Workspace Privacy Policy.
