Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to Jarvis Workspace, a private Google Workspace integration operated by Andrew Barber for his personal use. Jarvis Workspace is not offered to the general public.

Google user data accessed

Jarvis requests read-only OAuth scopes for Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts. Depending on the assistance requested, it may access:

  • calendar lists, event details, availability, locations, attendees, and descriptions;
  • email messages, threads, headers, metadata, labels, and attachments;
  • files and metadata available through Google Drive, including Google Docs and Sheets content; and
  • contact names, addresses, telephone numbers, and related contact fields.

These permissions do not allow Jarvis to send email or create, modify, share, or delete Google Workspace content.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide assistance requested by Andrew, including answering scheduling questions, summarizing upcoming commitments, locating and summarizing communications or documents, supporting research and planning, and retrieving contact information.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, used to build advertising profiles, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence models by the operator.

Processing and disclosure

Jarvis runs through a privately controlled Hermes Agent installation. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator’s Mac Studio. When assistance is requested, the Google user data needed to answer that request may be processed by the configured artificial-intelligence service provider and may appear in local Hermes session records.

Data is disclosed only to infrastructure or artificial-intelligence service providers needed to perform the requested assistance, or when required by law. It is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Storage, retention, and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally with access restricted to the operating account. Google user data is retrieved as needed. Relevant content may be retained in local session records, task artifacts, or user-directed notes until deleted as part of system maintenance or at the operator’s request.

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including profile-scoped credential storage and least-privilege read-only permissions, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis Workspace’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

Google access can be revoked at any time through the authorizing account’s Google Account connections. The operator can also delete the locally stored OAuth token and relevant local session records or task artifacts.

Changes

This policy will be updated if Jarvis Workspace’s data access or handling practices materially change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be submitted through the Jarvis Workspace GitHub repository.
